## Which domain terms does a newcomer need, and what does each mean here?

- **Always on**: applied every session and prompt without typing the command.
- **The one command**: the skill, shown as `/i-have-headache:i-have-headache` (Claude Code), `$i-have-headache` (Codex), `/i-have-headache` (Cursor).
- **Shipped path**: a path whose change must bump the version ([rule](../../.ai/rules/version-discipline.md)).
- **Loud contract**: the all-caps headline plus the numeric limit (at most 5 short bullets).
